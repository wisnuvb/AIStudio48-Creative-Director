# Overview

AI Studio 48 Creative Director Knowledge Repository is a structured knowledge base that captures how professional Creative Directors think, make decisions, evaluate ideas, and guide creative video projects.

**Creative Video** is the primary domain: ads, short movies, brand films, social content, documentaries, and other creative formats. Advertising is one format within this scope—not the whole discipline.

This repository is **not** a collection of prompts.

This repository is **not** an AI chatbot.

This repository is the knowledge foundation that will eventually power the AI Studio 48 Creative Director System.

The long-term goal is to transform expert reasoning into reusable workflows that can later be implemented in software.

---

# Vision

Create an AI system that does not simply generate video content, but understands how experienced Creative Directors solve business and brand problems through creative video.

The system should be able to:

* Understand business objectives.
* Analyze target audiences.
* Discover human insights.
* Develop creative strategies.
* Evaluate creative video ideas across formats.
* Review creative quality.
* Continuously improve through structured reasoning.

---

# Mission

Reverse engineer the thinking process of world-class Creative Directors and transform their expertise into reusable knowledge.

Instead of teaching AI **what to create**, we teach AI **how experts think before creating**.

---

# Problem Statement

Most AI video tools focus on content generation.

```
Prompt
    ↓
Image
    ↓
Video
```

This approach skips the most valuable part of creative video work:

* Strategic thinking
* Consumer psychology
* Business reasoning
* Creative judgment
* Decision making

As a result, outputs often look impressive but fail to solve real business problems.

---

# Design Principles

## Reasoning First

Focus on thinking before generating.

---

## Decision Driven

Every workflow must include clear decision criteria.

---

## Evidence Based

Whenever possible, decisions should be supported by research, case studies, or industry best practices.

---

## Reusable

Knowledge should be independent from specific brands or individual projects.

---

## Version Controlled

Knowledge evolves over time.

Every important document must include:

* Version
* Status
* Last Updated
* Reviewer
